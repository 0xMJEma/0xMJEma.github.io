Privacy Policy

Game: Pinball Breaker  |  Developer: Mini Nuke  |  Effective Date: November 4, 2026

Mini Nuke ("we," "our," or "us") built the Pinball Breaker mobile game as a commercial/ad-supported application. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you play Pinball Breaker.

Summary: Mini Nuke does not operate external backend servers and does not directly collect, store, or have access to your personal data. All online features, in-app purchases, and advertisements in Pinball Breaker are powered by official Google services (Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing, and Google AdMob), which process data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not directly collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information on our own servers. However, Pinball Breaker requires an active internet connection for Google Play Games Services and integrates third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) provided by Google that collect data to provide core functionality, process transactions, and display advertisements.

A. Google Play Games Services

Pinball Breaker uses Google Play Games Services to provide online features such as player authentication, leaderboards, achievements, cloud saves, and player statistics. When you sign in with your Google Play Games account, Google may collect and process:

  • Your Google Play Games profile information (Gamer ID, display name, and avatar).
  • Gameplay data (scores, achievements, saved game states, and session progress).
  • Device identifiers and diagnostic logs required for authentication and anti-abuse protection.

B. Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

Pinball Breaker offers in-app purchases processed securely through Google Play Billing. Mini Nuke never collects, receives, or stores your credit card numbers, bank account details, or billing address. Google processes the payment transaction and provides the game only with a non-personal purchase token and order status to unlock your purchased content.

C. Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the game. To serve personalized or non-personalized ads and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • Device and Account Identifiers: Your device's Advertising ID (AAID) and app set ID.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse location derived from your IP address.
  • App Activity and Ad Interactions: Information on how ads are displayed and interacted with.
  • Diagnostics and Performance Data: Crash logs, launch times, and device specifications (device model, OS version, screen dimensions).

2. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Because data processing is handled directly by Google's services, we encourage you to review their official privacy policies and terms:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services
  • Google Play Games Services Privacy & Terms
  • Google AdMob & AdSense Privacy Policy

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Mini Nuke does not store any user data on external servers. All data retention and deletion are managed either locally on your device or directly through your Google Account:

  1. Deleting Local Game Data: You can erase all locally stored game preferences and cache at any time by clearing the app storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling Pinball Breaker.
  2. Deleting Google Play Games Data: You have full control over the data stored by Google Play Games Services (including your scores, achievements, cloud saves, and sign-in connection). To delete your Pinball Breaker game data:
    • Open the Google Play Games app on your Android device.
    • Tap the three dots (More) at the top of the screen and select Settings.
    • Tap Delete Play Games account & data.
    • Under "Delete individual game data," find Pinball Breaker and tap Delete (or choose to delete your entire Play Games profile).
  3. Revoking Account Access: You can also manage or revoke Pinball Breaker's access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Account Permissions page.

4. Managing Advertising Preferences (Opt-Out)

You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your device settings:

  • Open your device's Settings → Google → All services (if applicable) → Ads.
  • Select Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID to limit ad tracking.

Where required by applicable regional laws (such as GDPR in the European Economic Area/UK or state privacy laws in the United States), a consent prompt may also be presented in-game allowing you to manage your ad personalization preferences.

5. Security

All data transmitted between Pinball Breaker and Google's third-party services (Play Games Services, Play Billing, and AdMob) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).

6. Children's Privacy

Pinball Breaker is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum legal age of consent in your jurisdiction). Mini Nuke does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data through Google's services while playing our game, please manage their account settings via Google Family Link or contact us so we can assist you.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our game features, third-party SDKs, or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date" at the top.
