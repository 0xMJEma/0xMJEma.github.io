Checkmate Protocol is a paid offline chess game with optional LAN multiplayer and Google Play Games Services integration. We do not operate our own online servers, advertising systems, analytics platforms, or account systems. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.
01

INFORMATION WE PROCESS

We do not independently collect, store, sell, or monetize personal information through servers operated by Sector VII.

Certain information may be processed locally on your device or by third-party platform providers necessary for core functionality. This may include:

  • Local game save data stored on your device
  • Gameplay progress and achievements
  • Google Play Games Services account identifiers
  • Local network connection data required for LAN multiplayer

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or behavioral tracking.

02

GOOGLE PLAY GAMES SERVICES

The App integrates Google Play Games Services ("GPGS"), provided by Google, to support player authentication, cloud saves, achievements, leaderboards, and gameplay services. When a user signs in with Google Play Games Services, the App may access and use the following Google user data:

  • Google Play Games player ID
  • Google account profile name
  • Google account profile avatar/image
  • Saved game data and cloud save information
  • Achievement progress and achievement status
  • Leaderboard scores and ranking data
  • Basic gameplay profile information required for Google Play Games functionality

This data is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Authenticating players into Google Play Games Services
  • Synchronizing cloud save progress between user devices
  • Displaying achievements and achievement progress
  • Submitting and retrieving leaderboard scores
  • Maintaining gameplay-related Play Games functionality
  • Enabling optional Google Play Games features requested by the user

Sector VII does not use Google user data for:

Selling or transferring Google user data to third parties
Advertising, marketing, or behavioral profiling
Analytics beyond what is necessary for GPGS functionality
Training artificial intelligence or machine learning models

Google processes Google Play Games Services data in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms. We do not receive or store your Google account password or direct Google account credentials.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
Google Play Games Services: developers.google.com/games/services

03

LAN MULTIPLAYER

The App includes optional peer-to-peer LAN multiplayer using local Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot connections. During LAN sessions:

  • Devices communicate directly with each other over the local network
  • Temporary local network data such as local IP addresses and session data may be used to establish and maintain gameplay connections
  • Multiplayer traffic is not routed through servers operated by Sector VII

We do not operate matchmaking servers, online game servers, or centralized multiplayer infrastructure.

04

PERMISSIONS

The App may request the following Android permissions:

PERMISSION PURPOSE
INTERNET Required for Google Play Games Services functionality and LAN multiplayer connectivity
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used to determine network availability for multiplayer and service connectivity

The App does not access contacts, photos, microphone data, precise location data, or unrelated device information.

05

DATA STORAGE AND RETENTION

Game save data may be stored locally on your device or through Google Play Games Services cloud save functionality, if enabled. Locally stored data remains on your device unless removed by uninstalling the App or clearing application data. Cloud save retention is managed by Google according to your account settings and GPGS policies.

We do not maintain independent databases containing player save files.
