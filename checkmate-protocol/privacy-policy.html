Privacy Policy

Checkmate Protocol
Developed and published by Sector VII
Last updated: May 20, 2025

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Checkmate Protocol ("the App") handles user information. We do not run our own servers or databases. However, the App uses Google Play Games Services, which does collect and process certain data on our behalf. This policy explains what that means for you.

Information Collected

Data We Collect Directly

We do not independently collect, store, or sell any personal information. The App does not transmit data to any servers operated by Sector VII.

Diagnostic and Performance Data

The App may generate crash reports and performance diagnostics (including CPU and RAM usage data and bot AI performance logs) when the App encounters an error. This data is surfaced to us through the Google Play Console and is used solely to identify and fix technical issues. It is not linked to your identity.

Google Play Games Services

The App integrates Google Play Games Services (GPGS), a platform provided by Google LLC. When you sign in with GPGS, Google may collect and process the following:

  • Your Google account display name and profile picture
  • A unique player ID assigned by Google
  • Game save data (used to save and load your game progress)
  • Achievements and gameplay statistics

This data is processed by Google under their own Privacy Policy. We do not have direct access to your Google account credentials. Save data stored via GPGS is accessible to us only in aggregate or as required to operate the save/load feature.

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How We Use Information

  • Crash and performance data is used exclusively to diagnose and fix bugs.
  • GPGS data is used to authenticate players and enable save/load functionality.
  • We do not use any data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated into the App is Google Play Games Services. We do not use any advertising SDKs, analytics platforms, or other third-party data processors.

Advertising

The App contains no advertisements.

Data Retention and Deletion

Crash and performance logs are retained by Google Play Console for a limited period as determined by Google. GPGS save data is retained until you delete it through your Google account settings or uninstall the App. You can manage or delete your Google Play Games data at any time via your Google account at myaccount.google.com.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through GPGS, please contact Google directly to request deletion.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it. Since the data processed through GPGS is governed by Google, requests related to that data should be directed to Google. For any other privacy concerns related to this App, contact us directly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
