1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Mini Nuke ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with BlocBoy ("the App").

BlocBoy is an offline single-player game for Android devices. The App does not require user registration, does not operate online multiplayer services, and does not use cloud-based game saves.

We do not independently collect, maintain, or store personal information on servers operated by Mini Nuke. However, the App integrates Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process certain information through this service.

By using the App, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect and Process

2.1 Information Stored Locally

BlocBoy stores basic game-related information locally on your Android device. This information may include:

  • Game progress and saved gameplay data.
  • High scores and other game statistics.
  • Game settings and preferences.
  • Other information necessary for normal game functionality.

This information is stored in a local save file on your device. It is not automatically transmitted to Mini Nuke or uploaded to a cloud storage service by the App.

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. Uninstalling the App may permanently delete locally stored progress.

2.2 Information Processed by Advertising Services

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to display optional rewarded advertisements.

Although Mini Nuke does not independently collect personal information, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain information, including:

  • IP addresses, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Android Advertising ID and other device or advertising identifiers.
  • App interactions, including app launches, advertisement views and taps.
  • Device information and technical characteristics.
  • Diagnostic information, including SDK and application performance data.
  • Information necessary for advertising delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

The exact information processed may depend on the user's device, Android settings, advertising configuration, applicable consent requirements and the features provided by Google.

3. Google AdMob and Advertising

BlocBoy uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display rewarded advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements are optional. Users may choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game reward where that functionality is available.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may use collected information for purposes including:

  • Delivering and displaying advertisements.
  • Providing personalized or non-personalized advertisements, as applicable.
  • Measuring advertising performance and engagement.
  • Preventing invalid advertising activity, fraud and abuse.
  • Maintaining and improving advertising services.

Depending on applicable laws, user consent and advertising configuration, advertisements may be personalized using information associated with the device or advertising activity. Non-personalized advertisements may also be displayed.

Google and its advertising partners may receive, process and share information as necessary to provide their advertising services, subject to their respective privacy policies and applicable laws. Mini Nuke does not independently control these third-party processing activities.

For more information about Google's data practices, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK Data Disclosure

4. Advertising Preferences and Consent

Users may have options to manage advertising preferences through their Android device settings, Google's advertising settings, or consent-management interfaces presented by Google where required by applicable law.

Depending on the device and applicable regulations, users may be able to reset or delete their Android Advertising ID or adjust advertising privacy settings.

Where legally required, advertising consent and privacy choices are handled through the applicable consent mechanisms integrated with the advertising service.

Users in jurisdictions that provide additional privacy rights may also exercise those rights through the relevant Google privacy and advertising controls.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Mini Nuke does not sell, rent, or independently disclose personal information to third parties for its own commercial purposes.

Information may nevertheless be processed or shared by Google and its advertising partners through the Google Mobile Ads SDK as necessary to provide advertisements, measure performance, prevent fraud and operate advertising services.

We may also disclose information if required by applicable law, regulation, legal process or a valid governmental request.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Game-related information is stored locally on the user's device in a save file. Mini Nuke does not maintain a remote database of player profiles, gameplay progress or save files.

Locally stored game data remains on the device until it is deleted by the user, removed through the Android application settings, or deleted when the App is uninstalled, subject to the device's operating system behavior.

Information processed by Google AdMob is retained and managed according to Google's applicable data retention practices and privacy policies. Mini Nuke does not independently control Google's retention periods.

7. Data Security

Since BlocBoy does not operate its own servers or maintain centralized player databases, game progress is primarily maintained on the user's device.

We take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection and transmission of information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's security practices and safeguards.

8. Children's Privacy

BlocBoy is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed or directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through systems or servers operated by Mini Nuke.

Advertising services integrated into the App may process device and advertising-related information. Advertising configurations, content and consent mechanisms must comply with applicable children's privacy and advertising requirements.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us directly, please contact us so that we can address the matter appropriately.
